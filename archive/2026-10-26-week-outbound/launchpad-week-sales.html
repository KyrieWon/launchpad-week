FAQ

Who pays directory fees?

$99 = our work only. Directory platform fees are separate — e.g. TAAFT Basic $49 or Max $437 one-time (you pay the platform; fees change, confirm on their site). See Which directories?.

When do we start?

Pay → submit intake → we email a brief to confirm → clock starts on lock. No live call required. Usually within 1 business day (Asia/Shanghai). Delivery: shared Google Drive + email for approvals.

What if a directory rejects the listing?

You still get the pack, checklist, assets, and proof log of what we filed. Acceptance isn’t guaranteed — filing is.

Is Outbound included?

No. Launchpad Outbound ($499/wk) is optional: ICP list + approved sends + reply log. Cancel anytime.

What timezone?

Asia/Shanghai (UTC+8). Brief lock and delivery follow that zone unless noted.

How do I get deliverables?

Shared Google Drive folder + email for approvals. Proof log on Day 7. (Notion is not the default.)

What if I don’t confirm the brief?

We nudge twice by email. If there’s still no confirmation, we pause the week. Full refund is available before the emailed brief is locked.

Which directories?

Default TAAFT (you choose Basic $49 or Max $437 — you pay the platform). Other directories only if agreed in the emailed brief; you still pay their fees. Fees change; confirm on their site.
